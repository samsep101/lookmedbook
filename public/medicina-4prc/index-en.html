Specific features of In vitro fertilization in Russia

In vitro fertilization (IVF) procedures in Russia are absolute legal. All IVF procedures are strictly controlled by the Russian legislation, and the clinic must have a license to engage in this activity.

Based on expert estimates, prices of IVF procedures in Russia are at least 20% lower than in some European countries and 2 to 3 times lower than in USA.

Number of babies born in Russia with the help of IVF is more than 25 000 per year.

As opposed to some other countries, in Russia a donor can be anonymous.

As a rule, IVF procedure takes from 28 to 39 days.

1

Preparation. The couple should undergo the complex medical examination, including evaluation of reproductive status, which enables the doctor to define the possibility of conception.

2

For the first 14–25 days, protocol of multifollicular ovarian stimulation is carried out. During this period the woman receives hormonal drugs intensifying the work of ovaries in order at once several ova were ready.

3

Then the specialists begin receiving of ova using the puncture method. That same day the man donated his sperm. In the laboratory the specialists examine and select healthy and viable eggs and sperm cells. On the 3rd–5th day after receiving the materials, the specialists carry out invitro fertilization and USG-guided placement of the embryos into the uterus. The embryos are placed transvaginal with the aid of a special catheter. After the implantation the specialist prescribed the woman special supportive drugs.

4

During the last period of 14 days the couple waits for pregnancy.
